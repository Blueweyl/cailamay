CAILAMAY

Stay where the noise cannot reach you.

A private tropical residence created for two people to slow down, reconnect and experience Palawan without the crowds.

THE VILLA

Designed around two people.

One low horizontal structure, set back into the jungle so the sea arrives before the architecture does. Glass walls retract entirely; the limestone floor runs from the sofa to the pool edge without a threshold. There is no lobby, no corridor of doors, no neighbour.

Rooms are open on two sides and closed on the others — you can spend a whole day undressed, unhurried and unseen.

MORNING

Wake slowly.

Curtains open on their own schedule. Coffee arrives on the terrace, the tide is out, and the water in the cove is the colour of glass. Nothing is scheduled until you schedule it.

EVENING

Stay a little longer.

One table is set beside the pool as the sun drops behind the islands. Dinner is cooked for two and served once, quietly, then the house is yours again.

Only yours.

Small arrangements, quietly made. No packages, no announcements — the villa simply notices what two people need.

THE COVE BELOW THE HOUSE

At the edge of Palawan.

Limestone rises straight out of the water here, holding the coastline in a series of coves that boats never bother to enter.

Mornings are still enough to hear the forest before the sea. By late afternoon the water goes flat and the islands turn to silhouettes.

Everything worth seeing is a short boat ride away — and nothing worth seeing requires you to leave the house.
